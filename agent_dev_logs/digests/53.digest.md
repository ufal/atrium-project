The contract is the same everywhere:
- an over-limit input is refused with `{status, reason: "limit_exceeded", detail, limit}` (413 / 422 / 504), or processed in full with a `limits_applied` note;
- a full service answers 429 `busy` with `Retry-After`;
- `/info` gives `limits` and `limits_meta`;
- a malformed value stops the service at startup and names the variable.
