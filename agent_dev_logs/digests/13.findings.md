## Must-fix

  pages according to their visual content, and grades each recognised line before enrichment."
  Evidence: the route starts with digital-convert's per-page verdict; page classification runs only on the pages that
  need the ATR and never routes (H3, H4); every line is scored, born-digital ones too (D17).
  Suggest: "…therefore first checks the text of every page, describes the pages that need recognition by their
  visual content, and grades every line before enrichment."
- **M2 — l.208, Table 1, page-classification output.** Now: "Page-level categories and confidence scores for routing
  and later content analysis". Evidence: H3; Ronald and motyc on 11-17 (ask 6): the categories are not sent to the
  ATR (D33 resolved). Suggest: "…for describing the pages and for later content analysis".

  service, not the translated text". Evidence: in the pilot the translator writes only `metadata-en` and its

  scope). Suggest: "…; the record is not changed, and the paradata of the run name the service and the fields
  translated."
- **M4 — l.256, Figure 2 caption.** Now: "…reading views are derived from the record." Evidence: motyc's list of
  11-18 23:57 on #13 (the last caption change); l.247 says the views are regenerated "from the layout

  representation."
- **M5 — `PCJ-sample.bib`, the six `@software` entries.** Now: `date = {2026-11-04}`, no version. Evidence: l.422
  says the services "are cited at the releases set as the starting point of the AMCR pilot"; that set is
