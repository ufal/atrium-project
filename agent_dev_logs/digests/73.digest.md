## 🎯 Goal
Two record-level outputs for the S3 discovery scope, and nothing broader:
1. **A statistical-keyword block**: KeyBERT by default, YAKE selectable, method and score kept with each keyword,
   distinct from the controlled vocabulary keywords; usable by discovery without depending on LLM output.
2. **An optional document-level quality summary**, after the page and line baseline is stable: derived
   deterministically from the page and line quality already in the record, no second quality model, page quality
   staying the authoritative stored signal, **no new gating**.

The issue's acceptance criteria, in full: the record distinguishes controlled and statistical keywords; statistical
keywords carry method and score; discovery consumers can use the block without LLM-generated vocabulary; the summary is
deterministic and derived from the record; no new quality gating; compatible with the S3 scope (lemma search, entity
facets, keywords, configurable OCR-quality bands). Deferred by the issue: custom NER and annotation tooling, broader
document understanding, vocabulary-gap experiments.

## ✅ What's settled
- **Two kinds, both kept, apart** (report §5): controlled from the vocabulary step, statistical with method and score.
- **KeyBERT by default, YAKE selectable** (report §5, item 12).
- **The summary is last, derived, and never a gate**; page quality stays the stored signal; bands belong to the index.
- **The owner is keyword-extractor** (nlp-enrich#40, after the meeting), for both kinds.
- **The block is additive** under `doc-schema-v1`'s rules, and shares one schema round with #71's `sha512`.

## ⏳ Open
1. **The block's shape**: document and per-page keywords, and where the method's parameters are recorded.
2. **Scores across methods**: one `score` field with three meanings, or a declared scale per method.
3. **Who writes the summary**: a stored block from the stage that scores lines, or a function consumers call at index
   time.
4. **Per-page keywords**: wanted by the Digital Archive's per-page index, or document keywords only for S3?
