## ✅ What is settled

1. **No namespace request from #51.** Source concepts keep their AMČR/TEATER URIs. Only ATRIUM-authored concepts use `SKOS_BASE = "https://w3id.org/atrium/"`, and the redirect (F5) is optional.
2. **The hub registry** (`docs/templates/shared/atrium_vocab.py`, self-test: 6 schemes, 64 concepts, 5 collections, 74 mappings) is the authority for page categories, line categories (two originators), quality bands, entity types/CNEC and the 11 harmonisation themes.
3. **The source SKOS view.** `union.skos.ttl` has 5,594 concepts (1,460 AMČR + 4,134 TEATER) and 55,188 triples. It is deterministic and drift-checked by `vocab_build.py --check`, and released in the asset.
4. **Semantics, explicitly:**
   - AMČR `hierarchie_vyse` is `skos:related` (all measured edges cross heslář boundaries);
   - TEATER gets one direct `skos:broader` plus `broaderTransitive`;
   - AAT quotations are `dcterms:source`, not identity;
   - `same_as` is a label heuristic, not `owl:sameAs`.
5. **Provenance without changing the prompt.** `resolve_pid()` fills `entities[].pid`, and `teater_category_ids` carries source identity per item. Neither enters the nested prompt.
