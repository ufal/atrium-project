**Forced labels, in short:** decoding under the label enum makes the model finish whichever label shares the prefix it began. That gives `Artemísion` 94 times, `zvonice` for Bell Beaker, and `Pentelikon` for *peníz*. The case-folding fix `2bd5db4` reaches only the capitalised cases.
