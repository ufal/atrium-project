## 🎯 Goal
Make the five published service images deployable unattended on ARÚP/ARÚB's Kubernetes: every
Dockerfile declares a `HEALTHCHECK` wired to the existing `/health` endpoint, and every service
handles `SIGTERM` for a clean shutdown instead of being killed mid-request on a rolling restart.
