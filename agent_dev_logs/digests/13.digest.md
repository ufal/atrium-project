## The CAA route

From the issue body's links and the conference's instructions:
1. the paper goes to Zenodo first, in the `caa2026` community, with its data and code unless there is a reason not
   to;
2. it is submitted through PCI Archaeology, with **the chairs of the session where it was presented as
   recommenders**, and a cover letter saying it is for the CAA2026 Proceedings;
3. at most 5,000 words without the bibliography, in the PCI template (the Overleaf project uses it).

The talk, "Digitized Yet Hidden: FAIRifying Archaeological Archives through Vision and Language Models" (slides: Zenodo `10.5281/zenodo.19500212`), was given in **Session 11**, "Reframing Cultural Properties Rather than as ‘Relics of the Past’",
organised by **Fujita Haruhiro** (Niigata University of International and Information Studies) and **Kawano
Kazutaka** (Tokyo National Museum): the presentation file is titled "CAA conference presentation (S11)", and Kate
confirmed it on 10-21. An earlier draft named Session 38 and its chairs, from the ARIADNE and ATRIUM calls for
papers; that was wrong.

## The evidence the paper can use

- **The classifier figure: decided (Kate, 10-21).** The paper reports the `vX.4` models re-run on the test sets of
  the LINDAT dataset, which are held out from training by design; the run uses the page-classification fix of
  [#61](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-page-classification/issues/61) (on `test`, `v1.9.6-beta` prepared). The
  99.16 % of motyc's brief was measured on the 5,449-page evaluation set. That set was meant to be held out too, but
  the script that selected it (`result/stats/unused.sh`) missed zero-padded page numbers and ids with a letter, so
  4,296 of its pages are in fold 1's training partition (3,679 + 617; fixed on 10-21, see #15).
- **The figures are in (10-22):** top-1 94.64–95.87 % on the models' held-out folds, 93.87–95.60 % on pages of
  unseen documents, top-3 99.75–99.90 %. They go into Table 2 after the co-authors' review asked for on #15; the
  table is in the #15 brief, section 1.
- Everything else comes from the evidence register in the brief: tested components, the fixture-based end-to-end
  runs, and the pilot as planned work.
- The SSH Open Marketplace records are corrected and submitted on 22–24 October (#4, same milestone). After that
  the paper can cite them; before, it cites the hub pages.
