## 2026-11-09 — what the services' terms say about outputs

Asked: is the output only non-commercial? If so, the letter might be dropped (motyc, 10-21: "not sure we need that
in writing now"). Read on 11-09 from the live pages:

| Service               | Its page                                                                                                                                                                  | Points to           |
|-----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Translation (CUBBITT) | "available for personal and non-commercial use"                                                                                                                           | the Terms of Use    |
| UDPipe 2, NameTag 3   | "freely available for testing"; the models' CC BY-NC-SA; commercial exploitation needs the authors' written permission; ÚFAL may use the data sent to improve its systems | the models' licence |

- **[LINDAT Terms of Use](https://lindat.mff.cuni.cz/en/terms-of-use)** (last updated 14/03/2021), "Personal and
  Non-Commercial Use Limitation": the services are for personal, non-commercial use; no one may "distribute,
  transmit, display, perform, reproduce, publish, license, create derivative works from, transfer, or sell any
  information … obtained from the Services"; "a specific contract between you and LINDAT/CLARIAH-CZ might override
  the limitations". Commercial use goes through the [licence agreement](https://lindat.cz/form/licence-agreement).
- **So:** more than non-commercial. Publishing a translation, lemma or entity in an AMČR record is outside the
  terms without that contract, so the letter stays. UDPipe and NameTag are offered for testing, not production,
  and the data-reuse clause matters for documents that are not public.
