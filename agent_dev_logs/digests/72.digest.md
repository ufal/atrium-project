### AMČR's answers (motyc, 10-13 13:56) and what they change

1. **Vocabulary list: "align it with the keyword-extract so we do not curate two separate lists".**
   - The translator's `load_vocab.py --from-release` (`records_from_release`, `:562`) merges the asset's `amcr_flat.csv` and `teater_flat.csv`. That gives 4,952 pairs, and it ignores the asset's curated `vocabulary.csv` (4,715 pairs, keyword-extract's exclusions applied).
   - The curated file has two columns only. The translator needs five (`source_lemma`, `target_translation`, `source`, `source_id`, `uri`).
   - So the change is: keep the flat rows whose pair is in the curated file, make that the default, and regenerate `data_samples/vocabulary.csv`. The README's release-path paragraph ("the switch waits on AMČR accepting that list") is then out of date.
2. **The LLM backend: "translate directly with some capable LLM … alternate to the vanilla LINDAT translation … the vocab (including descriptions) as one of the inputs".**
   - So the translator's OpenAI-compatible backend (`processors/llm_translator.py`, in its production image) stays as a second LLM client by design. R7's "one LLM client" covers the keyword stage.
   - The backend injects bare `src = tgt` pairs (`:219-247`). The flat JSON carries descriptions (`note_cs`/`note_en`: 96 of 1,460 AMČR terms, 2,145 of 4,134 TEATER terms).
   - `/translate` never loads a vocabulary: `service/api.py:317` calls `get_backend(backend, vocab_path=None)`.
   - This work belongs to translator#4's bake-off (its "Qwen 3.8 + vocabulary" item of 09-30).

## 🎯 Goal

Independently deployable services, each image carrying its stage and approved shared components only. In short:
- one service per stage;
- governed shared layers (one reader, one LLM layer for the keyword stage, one vocabulary artefact);
- research off the release line, with `para-drift` over the shared layers;
- PyMuPDF replaced, CC0 vocabularies, the established service conventions;
- immutable, rule-protected release tags; the GPU runner deferred.

## ✅ Settled

- **Six production stages.** `docs/k8s_deployment.md` carries the map, with `ghcr.io/ufal/atrium-<tool>-api:<version>`.
- **Owners:** reader = ocr-postprocess (vendored, pinned); keyword LLM layer and vocabulary = keyword-extract; translation backends = the translator.
- **Tool-to-tool vendoring is drift-checked.** PyMuPDF is out of every image, and CC0 is declared.
- **Release events of note.** Immutability does not protect a tag before its release is published: digital-convert's `v1.1.0-beta` tag moved `59859ef` → `36cdd34` before publication. The planned tag ruleset refuses that for non-admins. GHCR tags are outside both mechanisms; the image digest in paradata records what ran.
