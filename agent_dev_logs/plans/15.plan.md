## 3. Submission process

1. After the venue is selected, create a journal-specific checklist from its current instructions; do not assume IJDL requirements transfer unchanged.
2. Assemble one complete revised manuscript and all required figures, supplements, declarations and availability statements.
3. Ask co-authors for a focused review of the completed version, especially methods, data rights, authorship, AI declaration and funding.
4. Obtain explicit approval from all authors for the final manuscript, author order, declarations and destination journal.
5. Have the agreed corresponding author submit the approved version. Record venue, date, submission identifier and confirmation in #15.
6. Keep the arXiv preprint unchanged unless a separate author-approved decision is made about updating it. A reference-only update was completed in June; it does not itself require another update now.
