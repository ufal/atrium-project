## 2. Audit and correct the manuscript

Use the current Overleaf source offered in the issue and compare it with the exact PDF/version last submitted to IJDL. Make one consolidated revision; do not change the arXiv preprint as a side effect.

### Scientific and methodological correctness

- [ ] Reconcile the stated five-fold cross-validation, 80/10/10 partitions and fixed 5,449-page held-out test set. Verify the stored splits and describe training, checkpoint selection, model selection and final evaluation accurately.
- [ ] State whether pages from the same source PDF can occur across partitions. If split by page, say so and limit claims about generalisation to unseen documents accordingly.
- [ ] Remove numerical Cohen's κ estimates if they were not computed. Describe the actual collaborative annotation, expert review and adjudication process, plus its limitations.
- [ ] Identify the exact historical model revisions and dataset revision used for each reported result; distinguish the original experiment from later `v*.4` retraining.
- [ ] Keep claims proportionate to evidence: small differences between top image-only models are not demonstrated statistically significant; CLIP's one-fold protocol differs from the five-fold comparison; agreement over 649,508 unlabelled pages is consistency, not accuracy. Treat runtime estimates as estimates unless measured reproducibly.

## 3. Submission process

1. After the venue is selected, create a journal-specific checklist from its current instructions; do not assume IJDL requirements transfer unchanged.
2. Assemble one complete revised manuscript and all required figures, supplements, declarations and availability statements.
3. Ask co-authors for a focused review of the completed version, especially methods, data rights, authorship, AI declaration and funding.
4. Obtain explicit approval from all authors for the final manuscript, author order, declarations and destination journal.
5. Have the agreed corresponding author submit the approved version. Record venue, date, submission identifier and confirmation in #15.
6. Keep the arXiv preprint unchanged unless a separate author-approved decision is made about updating it. A reference-only update was completed in June; it does not itself require another update now.

## Done criteria

- A suitable target journal is agreed and its current guidelines are checked.
- The methodological, dataset/licensing, interpretation, authorship, funding and declaration checklist is resolved or explicitly documented as inapplicable.
- All authors approve the exact submission version and metadata.
- The new submission receipt/identifier and date are recorded on #15.
- The IJDL status is not misrepresented as a manuscript rejection without an actual editorial decision.
