## Stale facts corrected

- `-ff`'s default is `png` (`[SETUP] files_format`), not `jpeg`.
- The command line refuses a `-rev` no `REVISION_TO_BASE_MODEL` key matches: `main` (although `-h` names it), `v1.2`
  and `v2.0`–`v2.2`. The old examples `run.py --hf -rev v2.0` and "`main`, `v2.0`, `vX.2` or `vX.3`" are gone.
- `averaging.py` is at `supplementary/scripts/`; the old commands called `python3 averaging.py`.
- `[TRAIN] lr` sets the learning rate (not only `epochs` and `log_step`); `[YOLO] yolo_lr0 = 0` takes it.
- The Hub push: `run.py` does not call `push_to_hub()`; the token comes from `HF_TOKEN`, `[HF] token` stays blank.
- The averaged ensemble table's vote columns are `V1.4 … V5.4` (not `VM.3`).
- Licences: `--eval` declares the dataset since `v1.9.9-beta` (its label is CC BY-NC 4.0, not MIT). The claims that
  "the output licence is computed per run" and that "trained weights inherit" the dataset's terms are replaced by the
  component list with sources and the hub's position (#6): the label is not the output's licence.
- "`v4.4` … the most accurate of its generation" (hub), "`v5.4` most accurate", "`v4.4` best performing small model"
  (service): the held-out evaluation shows no significant difference between models on the same fold.
- The hub guide's sample rows came from `result/tables/20260530-1220_model_v43_TOP-3.csv`, which no longer exists:
  replaced by made-up rows and links to the stored tables (no archive data copied, #16).
- The hub changelog stopped at `v1.9.2-beta`, its history said the published tables describe `vX.3`; both brought to
  `v1.9.10-beta` (`CITATION.cff:4`). The hub guide's image example `1.8.0-beta` became `1.9.10-beta`.
- The hub reference said the service "defines none of its own" variables; it has its limits (`tool_limits.py`).
  `GET /` answers a JSON welcome, not `index.html`.
- `RAW` tables are sorted by the category scores, not by `FILE` and `PAGE`.
- The progress line is `<i>-th batch  Processed <n> images in <t> min`, every 50 batches.
- The "Base model - size" table gave EfficientNetV2-S the revision `v2.X`; it is `v1.2`. `regnety_640.see` → `.seer`.
- The `transformers` 5.x note, cut off after "raise", is completed with the pin (`<5`) and the Dependabot rule.
