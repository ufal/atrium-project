## 2. Produce one coherent manuscript — outline agreed by 10-22; complete draft by 10-25

Aim for approximately **3,500–4,200 words**, comfortably under the 5,000-word limit. The article should read as a methodological contribution for archaeology/digital heritage, not as a compressed catalogue of repositories.

### Evidence and wording constraints

- Use existing results only. The reported **99.16% RegNetY-16GF accuracy on a stated 5,449-page evaluation set** may be retained with its protocol and limitations clearly described. Classifier accuracy does not validate every downstream component.
- Explicitly distinguish components independently implemented/tested, integration demonstrated in existing fixtures, and the still-planned AMČR production pilot (December 2026–January 2027).
- Do not say Galaxy or the complete production pipeline is already deployed. Do not describe the tools as categorically on-premises: some configurations use LINDAT or external language-model services.
- Correct the dataset distinctions: original annotated dataset `1-5959` includes 318 restricted pages; public licence-compatible derivative `1-6184` excludes them. Say exactly which dataset underlies each result.
- Avoid unsupported terms such as “FAIR-compliant”, “calibrated”, “seamless”, “state-of-the-art” and “high-throughput”. Do not initiate new experiments only to support the paper.
