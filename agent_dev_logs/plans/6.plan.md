## Context

- the per-repository tables are `para_config.txt`;
- the NC-SA routes are nlp-enrich's models and the translator's LINDAT route.

What is open is how a record's licence is derived. Today the most restrictive component of the run decides it, tools included. stranak objected on 10-01 that a GPL renderer does not make its output GPL. K4TEL's proposal of 10-02 (only components whose content enters the record decide) was answered by motyc on 10-19: not as a rule, since a model's licence need not apply to its outputs either; record evidence, policy and output rights separately.

## Strategic actions

### A — Evidence, policy and output rights (a proposal first; next round)
1. **Answered (motyc, 10-19):** the 10-02 rule is not adopted. A tool's or model's licence does not decide its output's; source rights and service terms may.
2. **The proposal he asked for**, posted on #6 for review before any shared module changes:
   - a versioned, machine-readable structure with a JSON Schema, compatible with RO-Crate 1.2 / Process Run Crate 0.5;
   - a component inventory that tells software, models, datasets, vocabularies and services apart, with versions, licences or terms, and evidence;
   - configurable processing policies, with the policy and its outcome recorded in paradata;
   - a separate, optional, attributed rights assertion per output; an unknown status stays unknown;
   - a migration for `provenance.license` and `license_detail`: historical values kept, marked as calculated;
   - `atrium_rocrate.py` stops exporting the calculated licence as the root dataset's;
   - REUSE conventions (`REUSE.toml`, SPDX) for in-repo files, and a structured inventory for external components.
3. **Then:** implement across the six tools and their consumers, and re-vendor with `scripts/revendor_shared.sh`. The schema freeze does not block it (motyc), provided every tool and consumer changes together.
4. LINDAT's terms stay with #78.
