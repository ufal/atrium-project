# #79 C: where the translator documentation moved

> 2026-10-25, on translator `test` (it lands with the next release after `v1.5.0-beta`). The README went from 1,230
> lines to 164 and follows `docs/templates/README.template.md`, with a "Start here" path and an emoji-marked
> documentation map (emoji in body text only; every heading is plain). Nothing was dropped except what the "Removed"
> list names: each section of the old README, of `docs/translation-backends.md` and of the hub's five translator
> pages (`docs_site/tools/translator/`, folded into the repository as decided on 10-21) lives at the place below, its
> text carried over with plain headings, repointed links and facts brought to the current code. Every docs page opens
> with who it is for and what it answers, and the long ones with a list of their sections. The README's opening,
> Quick start, How it works, Limits and the licence paragraph were rewritten; the licence wording follows
> atrium-project#6 (the `license` field is a calculated summary of the components, not the licence of the
> translation). `CONTRIBUTING.md` and `service/README.md` keep their content; their headings lost their emoji.
> No record text of the ARÚP/ARÚB samples was pasted into the docs (hub #16): the examples link to `data_samples/`,
> and the two log rows shown are invented.
