````markdown
Maps archival text onto controlled archaeological vocabularies with a large language model, and converts born-digital documents into the ATRIUM document record.

**Vocabulary keywords.** Each text line (CSV, TEITOK) or each passage of a whole document (TXT, Markdown) is tagged with a term from the AMČR heslář and the TEATER thesaurus, with Czech and English keywords and a confidence score. ````markdown

This workflow looks at every scanned page and assigns it one of eleven categories, so that each page can be sent to the processing chain that suits it before any of that processing is run. The steps are carried out by the ATRIUM page classifier (RER7Fw). In the AMČR text workflow (0xSpVP) this is the page-classification step. Full narrative: https://ufal.github.io/atrium-project/workflows/page-classification/
