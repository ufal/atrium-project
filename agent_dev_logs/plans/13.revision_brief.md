## What the paper argues

Digitisation keeps archaeological archives safe, but it does not make what they say findable or reusable. ATRIUM
links content classification, text extraction, text-quality assessment and enrichment into one modular workflow,
for scanned and born-digital documents alike. The paper reports what each part does and how it was tested. It is
not a report on a deployed and evaluated production system.

Working title: *Digitized Yet Hidden: A Modular Processing Workflow for Archaeological Archives*.

## Table 1: the six tools

Needs `\usepackage{booktabs,array}`. Compiled and checked on 2026-10-21.

```latex
\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{The six ATRIUM tools as steps of one workflow. The OCR/HTR engine is not one of them.}
\label{tab:tools}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.17\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.33\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.24\linewidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.18\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Tool & What it does & Reads $\rightarrow$ writes & Where it runs \\
\midrule
page-classification & sorts each scanned page into one of eleven content categories that decide its next treatment (OCR, handwriting recognition, table or image extraction) & page images $\rightarrow$ a category per page & local; GPU or CPU \\
digital-convert & reads born-digital files into the document record without OCR and flags pages that still need it & PDF, DOCX, ODT, XLSX, RTF $\rightarrow$ record & local \\
ocr-postprocess & puts OCR lines in reading order and labels each with its language and a quality category (Clear, Noisy, Trash, Non-text, Empty) & ALTO and other OCR output $\rightarrow$ graded lines & local; GPU for batch runs \\
nlp-enrich & adds lemmas, part-of-speech tags, syntax and named entities & lines $\rightarrow$ CoNLL-U, entities, TEITOK XML & LINDAT services, or self-hosted \\
keyword-extract & finds statistical keywords and maps text to the AMČR and TEATER vocabularies & lines $\rightarrow$ keywords and vocabulary terms & a language model, self-hosted or a hosted API \\
translator & translates text in XML documents, keeping their structure and the domain vocabulary & ALTO, AMČR records $\rightarrow$ the same XML in English & LINDAT, or a self-hosted model \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
```

ocr-postprocess can optionally re-read a page image with a vision-language model (GLM-4v-9b). If the paper
mentions that, say it in the text and keep the table as it is.
