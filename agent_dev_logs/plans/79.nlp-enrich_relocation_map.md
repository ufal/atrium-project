> 2026-10-25, for nlp-enrich `test` (it lands with the next release after `v1.1.3-beta`). The README went from 1,439
> lines to 149 and follows `docs/templates/README.template.md`. Nothing was dropped silently: each section of the old
> README lives at the place below, its text carried over with the headings plain, the 📎/📁/📂/📄/📑 markers gone,
> the relative links repointed and stale facts corrected (listed at the end). The README's opening, Quick start,
> How it works, Where it fits, Limits and the acknowledgements were rewritten. Each new page opens with who it is for
> and, when long, a list of its sections. `CONTRIBUTING.md` keeps its content; its headings lost their emoji.
