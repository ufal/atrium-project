## Context
The Digital Archive will index every page as a document of its own and facet it by keywords and quality (report §3.1,
item 12). Two things are missing from the record for that: **statistical keywords**, which exist today only in
nlp-enrich's HTTP response and its TEITOK header, and a **document-level view of quality**, which consumers now have to
compute from the pages themselves.

Three facts shape the plan:
1. **The owner moved.** After the meeting, both keyword kinds belong to `ufal/atrium-keyword-extractor`
   (nlp-enrich#40). The block is designed here; it is produced there. A new block with its owner from day one is an
   additive change — no re-attribution, no MAJOR bump.
2. **Quality bands are the index's, not the record's.** AMČR and the Digital Archive compute bands at index time from
   their own thresholds (report §5). The record already stores a per-page band from alto-postprocess's thresholds; the
   summary must not add another band — it carries numbers the index can band itself.
3. **Scores do not mean the same across methods.** KER counts occurrences, YAKE's inverted score is normalised per
   document, KeyBERT's is a cosine (`keywords.py:5-23`). A keyword carries its method so that no consumer compares
   scores across methods.

## Strategic actions

### A — The `keywords` block (hub, the schema round shared with #71)
1. **Shape** — one list, every keyword self-describing; the method's settings recorded once:
   ```json
   "keywords": {
     "items": [
       {"term": "hradiště", "lemma": "hradiště", "lang": "cs", "method": "keybert", "score": 0.71, "rank": 1, "page": null},
       {"term": "sonda", "lang": "cs", "method": "keybert", "score": 0.64, "rank": 1, "page": "3"}
     ],
     "methods": {"keybert": {"model": "<sentence-transformers id>", "ngram_range": [1, 2], "scale": "cosine 0-1"}}
   }
   ```
   - `page: null` is a document keyword; a page label is a page keyword (the per-page index, report §3.1);
   - `score` keeps the method's own scale, declared once in `methods.<m>.scale` (`count`, `normalised 0-1`,
     `cosine 0-1`); `rank` is what consumers compare across methods;
   - `lemma` when the method worked on lemmas (KER) or could look them up;
   - `term` is never a controlled-vocabulary concept: controlled keywords stay in `enrichment` (acceptance 1).
2. **Owner**: `keyword-extractor` in `BLOCK_OWNERS`, from the first day.
3. **The round** (`docs/document_schema.md`, *Changing the schema after the freeze*): the schema edit, its
   `POST_FREEZE_CHANGES` entry, a `## Changelog — <date>` section, the producer shapes in
   `tests/test_document_required.py` — **one date** with #71's `source.sha512` and seed profile, and the successor map
   (hub, *`doc-schema-v1` as the pilot baseline*). One re-vendor, one `v1` move.
4. **Documentation**: the block in `document_schema.md`'s ownership table, with the score scales and the rule "compare
   ranks, not scores, across methods".

### B — Producing it (keyword-extractor, nlp-enrich#40 §B)
1. `/extract_keywords` with `kind=statistical` writes the block: `method=keybert` by default (`DEFAULT_KW_METHOD`),
   `yake` and `legacy` selectable; document and per-page keywords in one call.
2. **Input** is the record after nlp-enrich: `lines[].text` for YAKE and KeyBERT, `lines[].lemma`/`upos` for KER; lines
   with `categ` Trash or Empty left out (report §5).
3. **The CLI default follows the service**: `keywords.py`'s `DEFAULT_METHOD` becomes `keybert` in keyword-extractor, so
   batch runs and the service agree.
4. **Until keyword-extractor exists**, nothing is built in nlp-enrich to be moved later; the hub round (A) does not wait
   for it.

### C — The document quality summary (last, optional)
1. **A pure function in the shared module**, `quality_summary(record)` in `atrium_document.py`: same record in, same
   summary out, no model, no thresholds of its own. Proposed content:
   ```json
   "quality": {
     "pages":  {"n": 42, "scored": 40, "mean": 0.83, "median": 0.87, "min": 0.12, "needs_ocr": 2},
     "lines":  {"n": 1830, "by_categ": {"Clear": 1500, "Noisy": 200, "Non-text": 20, "Empty": 80, "Trash": 30}},
     "derived_from": ["pages[].quality_score", "pages[].needs_ocr", "lines[].categ"],
     "method": "atrium-quality-summary/1"
   }
   ```
   No band anywhere in it (context point 2); `by_categ` counts both originators' label sets as they are.
2. **Stored by the stage that scores lines**: ocr-postprocess (alto-postprocess until atrium-alto-postprocess#56)
   calls it at `finalize()` — for OCR records and, after llm-enrich#10 W3, for born-digital ones. The block is written
   once per run, so it cannot drift from the pages it summarises.
3. **The alternative**, not storing it and letting the index call the same function, is equally deterministic; choose
   by whether AMČR wants it in the archived record (RO-Crate) or only in the index (question 2).
4. **Additive**, in the same schema round as A, or in a later one if the page/line baseline is not yet stable — the
   issue's order.

### D — Consumers
1. **TEITOK**: nlp-enrich's projection (`api_util/teitok_project.py:29-31`) reads the `keywords` block instead of its own
   CSVs and writes `@resp="#app-keyword-extractor"` with `@scheme="#kw-<method>"`; it runs as `/project_record` after
   keyword-extractor (nlp-enrich#40 §C.4).
2. **The Digital Archive**: a mapping note in `document_schema.md` for aiscr-digiarchiv-2#711/#714 — page keywords to the
   page document, document keywords to the parent, quality numbers to the index's band thresholds.
3. **RO-Crate**: nothing new; the blocks travel inside the record.

## Verification / done-criteria
Mapped onto the issue's acceptance criteria:
1. *The record distinguishes controlled and statistical keywords*: a record with both validates; `keywords` has no
   concept URIs and `enrichment` no statistical scores (a test in `tests/test_document_required.py`).
2. *Statistical keywords carry method and score*: the schema requires `method` and `score` on every item.
3. *Usable without LLM-generated vocabulary*: `kind=statistical` alone produces the block (keyword-extractor test).
4. *The summary is deterministic and derived*: `quality_summary(r) == quality_summary(r)` over the E2E fixtures, and it
   changes only when `pages[]` or `lines[]` change (hub test).
5. *No new gating*: no stage refuses, skips or routes on the summary (a grep-level test that nothing reads it but
   consumers).
6. *Compatible with the S3 scope*: the mapping note (D.2) reviewed by the Digital Archive developers.

## Questions
1. **Per-page keywords** in the record: wanted for S3's per-page index, or document keywords only?
2. **The summary**: stored in the archived record (recommended), or computed at index time only?
3. **Score scales**: is `rank` plus a declared `scale` enough for the index, or should keyword-extractor normalise every
   method to 0–1?
4. **KER (`legacy`)** in production, or research only?
