### E — Release trust (decides closure)
1. **Record the state.** `python scripts/release_trust.py status` over the six repositories plus the hub; attach the output to #72 (and #40).
2. **Immutable releases** in the three repositories where they are off (page-classification, translator, nlp-enrich), before their next tags (page-classification with its `CreateAction`, translator with `095c3b2`). page-classification `v1.9.2-beta` was published mutable on 2026-10-05.
3. **Tag rules (rulesets)** that forbid moving or deleting `v*` tags.
   - Immutable releases protect a tag only once its release is published.
   - digital-convert's `v1.1.0-beta` was moved from `59859ef` to `36cdd34` before publication, with immutability on. The planned ruleset refuses that for non-admins and logs an admin's move as a bypass (`bypass_mode: "always"`).
   - GHCR image tags (`1.1.0-beta` was promoted from `59859ef` first) are outside both; the image digest in paradata is the record of what ran.
4. **Branch rules** on the default branch and `test` of every repository (PRs, required checks, no force-push), as `docs/release_trust.md` sets them.
5. **SHA pins.**
   - `scripts/pin_hub_reusables.py` replaces `@v1` in every hub `uses:` caller with `@<sha>  # v1` plus a matching `hub-ref`.
   - Then turn on `workflow_lint.py --require-sha-pins`, which refuses `@v1` for every hub reusable.
   - Today every tool repository has seven `@v1` references.
6. ARUP-CAS forks mirror only immutable release tags (AMČR side; their forks are already re-made).
