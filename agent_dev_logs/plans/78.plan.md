# Strategy — Issue [#78](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/78): LINDAT services in the AMČR pilot

> _Written 2026-10-21. Evidence: [`../digests/78.digest.md`](../digests/78.digest.md). Due **11 November 2026**._

**Status**: ⏳ **Waiting on CU and AMČR; one technical item ÚFAL can do first.**

## 1. Permission (Pavel, by 11 November)

1. Pavel asks CU for a written permission for AMČR's use of the LINDAT translation, UDPipe 2 and NameTag 3
   services, in the pilot and after it, with the expected volume (the AMČR collections: about 113,000
   documents).
2. The letter, or its reference, goes on the issue. Until then, the pilot runs with LINDAT as today, and the
   paradata records every call.

## 2. Monitoring (ÚFAL, before the pilot)

1. **A scheduled live lane** per LINDAT-using tool (#81 tier 4): daily, a fixed set of short Czech texts,
   several requests each so that more than one server behind the load balancer is reached. It checks that
   numbers and vocabulary terms are kept, that the reply is in the target language, and that a known sentence
   keeps its expected lemmas and entities. A failure opens or updates one issue, as the E2E lanes do.
2. **The pilot's own signal:** the share of re-requested translation replies and of `upstream_unavailable`
   answers, read from the paradata of a day's runs. AMČR sees it in the runbook's results table (#77).
3. What it still cannot catch: a fluent wrong translation. That needs people reading samples, stated as a
   known limit.

## 3. Licence (AMČR, by 11 November)

1. David and Dana say whether CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 output fits the licence AMČR moves its records to.
2. If it does not: the translator switches to the self-hosted backend by configuration; nlp-enrich has no
   alternative, so its blocks would be kept out of the published records, or the rights question goes to the
   #6 rights assertion (AMČR asserts the rights of what it publishes).

## Done when

- [ ] The scheduled live lane runs for the translator and nlp-enrich, and its failures reach an issue.
- [ ] AMČR's licence answer is on the issue, with the configuration that follows from it.
