**Working abstract** (149 words; to be replaced by the manuscript's final abstract):

> Archaeological archives hold decades of excavation reports, drawings, photographs and correspondence.
> Digitisation has preserved much of this material as page images and born-digital files, but it has not made
> their content findable or reusable: a scanned report is still a picture of text. We describe a modular workflow,
> developed in the ATRIUM project for the archives of the Institutes of Archaeology of the Czech Academy of
> Sciences in Prague and Brno. It sorts pages by content type, extracts text from scanned and born-digital
> documents, assesses the quality and language of each line, and adds linguistic annotation, vocabulary-based
> keywords and translation. One document record carries the results and the provenance of every step. We report
> what each component does and how it was tested, including the evaluation of the page classifier on an annotated
> dataset of archival pages, and we discuss what remains before the workflow is used in production.
