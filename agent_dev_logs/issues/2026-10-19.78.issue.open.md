# [\#78 Issue](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/78) `open`: LINDAT services in the AMČR pilot: written permission, service monitoring, output licence
**Labels**: `question`, `integration`


#### <img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/65828620?u=80b39d3cb2967c52e1d4af28dcefc7a1efdfdc79&v=4" width="50">[K4TEL](https://github.com/K4TEL) opened issue at [2026-10-19 19:56](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/78):

Two of the six tools call LINDAT in the pilot chain: atrium-translator by default (CUBBITT, plus UDPipe when a vocabulary is applied) and atrium-nlp-enrich on every run (UDPipe 2, NameTag 3). On 30 September this question moved to 11 November.

To settle by 11 November:
- [ ] **Permission:** CU's written permission for AMČR's use of these services, in the pilot and after it.
- [ ] **Monitoring:** how service quality is watched. In September about a third of translation replies were unusable, traced to one server behind the load balancer. The translator now detects and re-requests such replies, and nlp-enrich reports an outage as `upstream_unavailable`, but neither catches plausible wrong output.
- [ ] **Licence:** LINDAT output is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Does that fit the licence AMČR moves its records to (ARUP-CAS/aiscr-webamcr#2590)?

If not:
- translator: Qwen3.8-27B, self-hosted through the `openai_compatible` backend (Apache-2.0), came close to LINDAT in the bake-off (ufal/atrium-translator#4);
- nlp-enrich: no alternative; the UDPipe and NameTag models are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 wherever they run.
