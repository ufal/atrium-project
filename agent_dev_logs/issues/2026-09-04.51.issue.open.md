#### <img src="https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/20186892?u=81493d22a8cb5fce679ac0e5e32fd98a04d777c6&v=4" width="50">[motyc](https://github.com/motyc) commented at [2026-10-21 22:42](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/51#issuecomment-6328182010):

@K4TEL — **I choose option 1: `enrichment.items[].teater_category` should continue to hold the source-vocabulary label, as it does now.**

The reason is primarily semantic, not merely backward compatibility. The AMČR/TEATER terms and the eleven ATRIUM harmonisation themes represent different levels of information and should not be substituted for each other.

I would suggest the following:

1. **Keep `teater_category` unchanged.** It holds the selected AMČR/TEATER vocabulary label (or the explicitly defined meta-text sentinel). Although the field name is historical and somewhat misleading, changing its meaning now would be worse than retaining the name.

2. **Keep `teater_category_ids` as the corresponding source references.** These are important for traceability and future interoperability. They must retain the source identities and must not be replaced with ATRIUM-minted identifiers. The current RO-Crate approach, using source URIs and not minting a `/theme/<source-label>` URI, is correct.

3. **Correct the registry and validation semantics.** `atrium_vocab.CONCEPT_SCHEMES["theme"]` should not imply that `teater_category` contains one of the eleven themes. Likewise, that field must not be validated against the theme label set. Where validation of the selected term is needed, it should use the actual versioned vocabulary offered to the model, including its permitted sentinel, rather than the theme list.

4. **Treat ATRIUM themes as separate derived classifications.** They may be determined from the vocabulary's curated taxonomy and placement rules when actually needed, but not inferred solely from a bare source label. This is particularly important because several source concepts may share a label, and the same label may have different meanings. If we later need to persist the theme in `atrium_document`, we can introduce an explicit optional field with a clearly defined derivation and versioned taxonomy reference. **There is no need to add it now solely to close V-5.**

5. **Keep the correction minimal and consistent.** The present document schema already describes `teater_category` correctly, and the RO-Crate exporter already follows the intended source-URI policy. Please fix the remaining inconsistency in the registry, validators (where applicable), `docs/skos_strategy.md` and the published SKOS documentation, and preserve the relevant regression tests. I do not see a reason to change the keyword-extraction output, redefine the field or introduce a schema-version bump just for this clarification.

For acceptance, please ensure that the examples still behave as intended:

- `kostel` remains a source label, with its associated AMČR/TEATER identifiers.
- No false ATRIUM URI such as `https://w3id.org/atrium/theme/kostel` is generated.
- Missing identifiers or the meta-text sentinel do not lead to invented concept URIs.
- Source concepts sharing a label are not automatically asserted to be equivalent merely because their labels match.

More generally, I want to preserve the distinction between ATRIUM's internal SKOS-based alignment and the **formal SKOSification and publication of the authoritative AMČR/TEATER vocabularies**. The latter is being handled separately outside the scope of ATRIUM.
