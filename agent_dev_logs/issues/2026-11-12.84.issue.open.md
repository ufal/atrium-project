### 1. Two levels instead of one record built by every tool

The meeting found that the ATR cannot produce reliable ALTO (only block level), that a document with any page needing ATR should go through the ATR whole, and that docproc's shape should be considered for the record. Taken together, we propose:

```text
 LEVEL 1  layout   atr/docproc-json       written by digital-convert (text-layer pages, office files)
                                          or by the ATR (whole document when any page needs ATR);
                                          one stored per document
 LEVEL 2  record   atrium/document-json   positional blocks built by ocr-postprocess from docproc;
                                          page-classification, scoring, nlp-enrich, keyword-extract
                                          add their blocks as today
```

- **digital-convert writes docproc**, with lines (the docproc schema gets optional `lines[]`), and gives a verdict for every page (`text_layer`, `needs_atr`). It never refuses a document and never decides from a sample: a page it cannot use is `needs_atr`. `needs_ocr` and `needs_ocr_reason` become `needs_atr` and its reason.
- If no page needs ATR, the stored docproc is digital-convert's. Otherwise the whole document goes through the ATR with the `needs_atr` pages forced, and the stored docproc is the ATR's.
- **ocr-postprocess builds the record** from docproc. With two docproc outputs (a mixed document) it keeps digital-convert's exact lines on good pages, links each line to the ATR block that contains it, takes ATR-sourced lines for the pages that needed ATR and for blocks with no digital-convert line (figure OCR, scanned inserts), and rebuilds `content` in docproc's reading order.
- One writer for the positional blocks means `source.origin` no longer selects a writer; it stays as a descriptive value (`digital`, `atr`, `legacy`).
- **No ALTO is produced or stored.** nlp-enrich takes its layout from the record or docproc instead of ALTO, and TEITOK remains the standard linguistic output, with its limits stated. This revises the three-format decision of [ufal/atrium-project#24](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/24).
- page-classification runs on the `needs_atr` pages only.

### 2. Record: `atrium/document-json`

- **No run data at all.** Removed: `provenance` (`contributors[]`, `license*`), `assembled`, `derived_from`, `regenerable`, and also the per-block `run_uuid` from your draft: it would change the record's checksum on every rerun and is ambiguous for blocks split by field. Which run wrote which blocks and fields is named by the paradata `result`.
- **The layout stays in docproc.** Removed from the record: `tables[]` (docproc's `cellGrid` holds the table; its text stays in the record as lines), `content.reading_order`, `lines[].group_id`, `lines[].style`, and the page verdicts `text_layer`, `needs_atr`, `needs_atr_reason`, `atr` (they live in the stored docproc's routing).
- **New, written by the record builder** (names are proposals):
  - `lines[].block` — the id of the docproc block the line belongs to;
  - `lines[].text_source` — `text-layer`, `atr` or `legacy` (converted PCSS ALTO);
  - `lines[].bbox_precision` — `measured`, `derived` or `absent`;
  - `pages[].content_status` — `text` or `empty`, with a row for every page of the original.
- **Removed as before:** `forms`. **Reserved, unused in the pilot:** `translations`, `entities[].translation_en`.
- **Coordinates.** Every box is in the displayed page's space: origin at the top-left of the CropBox, after `/Rotate`, in units of 1/72 inch × UserUnit. `pages[].canvas` carries the UserUnit factor and, for images docproc wraps into a PDF, the ratio back to the image's pixels; its default unit becomes `pt`. Ronald is asked to approve the same definition for docproc. Pages converted from PCSS ALTO whose original cannot be matched may stay in `px`.
- `pages[].page_index` (the physical page number) on every page row, joined with docproc `pageNumber`; printed labels stay in `pages[].page`.

Who writes what:

| Step | Fields |
|---|---|
| intake (pipeline) | `schema_version`, `record_type`, the seed fields |
| record builder (ocr-postprocess) | `source.{language, page_count, origin}`; `pages[].{page_index, page, canvas, content_status}`; `lines[].{page, line, text, bbox, block, text_source, bbox_precision}`; `content.text` |
| page-classification | `page_categories`; `pages[].{category, category_confidence}` |
| scoring (ocr-postprocess) | `lines[].{categ, quality_score, lang}`; `pages[].{quality_score, quality_band}`; `quality_summary` |
| nlp-enrich | `lines[].{lemma, upos, feats, teitok_ref}`; `pages[].teitok_surface`; `entities[]` |
| keyword-extract | `enrichment`; `keywords`; `entities[].pid` |
| store (pipeline) | nothing; validation against `doc-schema-v2` |

### 3. Scoring

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Every line in the record is scored, whatever its text source. |
| 2 | `categ` holds only the five categories. digital-convert's decode verdict (`Garbage`, `Inverted`) becomes a flag on docproc lines that scoring may read; `/score_record` no longer skips those lines. |
| 3 | `quality_score` is computed from the text alone: one model, one set of constants and one band scheme for every source. The producer's confidence stays in docproc as a separate signal. |
| 4 | The pipeline gets the document passes (the page-context corrections) that the evaluated batch runs, so stored scores match the measured ones. |
| 5 | The unit is a visual line. If the ATR's blocks keep no line breaks, the block is scored as one unit, marked as such, and kept out of calibrated statistics until point 8. |
| 6 | `Trash` triggers no re-route in the pipeline; re-processing is a later, separate campaign. |
| 7 | Empty pages have no lines and no page score; `quality_summary` counts them separately. |
| 8 | The rules stay frozen for the pilot; their validity on ATR text and on text-layer text is measured on the documents that have both PCSS lines and an ATR run, before the production version is fixed. |

### 4. Paradata

As in your draft, plus:

- Inputs and outputs are named by AMČR distribution name and SHA-512, never by `ni:///sha-256` identifiers or local paths.
- A run whose output is not stored but reaches a stored distribution (digital-convert's docproc of a mixed document) is recorded with that output's SHA-512, explicitly marked as not stored; "no outputs that are not stored" becomes "no unmarked outputs that are not stored".
- `result` names the record blocks **and fields** each action wrote.
- `actionStatus` stays: what AMČR stores is always completed, and development and tests use the same frame.
- digital-convert's paradata go to `paradata/atr/docproc-json` when its docproc is stored, otherwise into `paradata/atrium/document-json` as above.

### 5. Translator

The service takes the AMČR field list **with each request**. There is no default list (`amcr-fields.txt` and the `fields =` key go); in metadata mode a request without a list is refused. The list used is recorded in `paradata/metadata-en` `parameters`, with no field text.

### 6. Licences

Unchanged from your draft and [ufal/atrium-project#6](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/6): no calculated licence anywhere — not in the record, the paradata or a crate — and no licence merging (`merge_effective_licenses`) in the templates or the validator; each run lists its components with their terms. The LINDAT terms for nlp-enrich and the translator ([ufal/atrium-project#78](https://github.com/ufal/atrium-project/issues/78)) are settled at the next meeting; until then their outputs are not published.

### 7. docproc (`atr/docproc-json`)

Ronald is asked to move the schema to `ARUP-CAS/aiscr-docs-pipeline` under an ARUP-CAS identifier; ARÚ/B decides on changes, and both the ATR and ÚFAL propose them. Compared with your draft: `metadata.routing` **stays in the output** (it is a signal about the content, not run data); empty pages have no `pages[]` entry, but routing lists every page of the original, an empty one with route `empty`; the ATR echoes the forced pages in routing with their reason.

### Per tool, in short

| Tool | Change |
|---|---|
| digital-convert | writes docproc with lines and per-page verdicts; no refusal, no sampling; `needs_atr` |
| ocr-postprocess | builds the record from docproc (merge for mixed documents); scoring rules 1–8 |
| nlp-enrich | layout from the record or docproc, not ALTO |
| page-classification | runs on `needs_atr` pages; unchanged output |
| translator | field list per request, no default |
| shared templates and validator | the record shape of section 2; paradata of section 4 |

Questions and counter-proposals are welcome here; for anything that changes AMČR data or its contract, ARÚP decides.
