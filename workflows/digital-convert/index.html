    Reading born-digital documents into their layout, without OCR¶

    The digital-convert workflow. Tool: atrium-digital-convert. The tool was part of llm-enrich until 1 October 2026; it continues in a new repository under its own name, and the keyword extraction that llm-enrich also did moved to keyword-extract.

    Scope

    This page gives the stable core of the workflow: its purpose, its steps, the formats it reads and writes, and the licences of the components it uses. The converter's engines and options, the rules that give a page its verdict, and the service's limits and settings with their defaults are documented with the code — in the tool's README, whose documentation map leads to its usage, configuration and how-it-works pages, and its service README — because they change as the rules are calibrated on AMČR documents.

    Purpose¶

    A PDF, a word-processor file or a spreadsheet that was never printed and scanned already carries its text; running OCR on it would only add errors. This workflow reads such a file directly and writes its layout as docproc (atr/docproc-json), the format the ATR, AIS CR's text-recognition service, writes for scanned pages: the pages, the blocks in reading order with their lines (with their boxes, in a PDF or an office document), the headings, running headers and footers, footnotes and tables. It also checks the text layer of every page. A page whose layer is missing, does not decode to readable text or comes from an earlier OCR run is marked needsAtr, so that the whole document goes through the ATR with those pages forced; so is an office document's page with no text of its own (none outside its header and footer) that shows a picture, such as a pasted scan. No document is refused for its pages. ocr-postprocess builds the ATRIUM document record from the docproc.

    At a glance¶
    In PDF, office documents (DOCX, DOC, ODT, RTF) read as their own text on the pages of a LibreOffice rendering, and, without pages to render, spreadsheets (ODS, XLSX, XLS) and text files (TXT, CSV, TSV, JSON), recognised by their content, not their extension; images go to the ATR instead; optionally AMČR's seed of the record (the document's identifier and the checksum of its original)
    Out the docproc (JSON), with a verdict on every page's text layer, the route of every page and a figure block for every picture, chart or drawing; an office document's rendered PDF when the ATR or page-classification reads it, not stored (the paradata name it by its SHA-512); on request, the document as annotated Markdown; the run's paradata (JSON)
    Runs as an HTTP service, api-digital (image -api: POST /reformat), and a command-line tool (api_util/digital_to_json.py, image -digital)
    Compute CPU, no GPU; memory follows the text a document holds, not its file size; the optional layout-model engine for complex PDFs is a local, opt-in build
    Network none; the optional layout-model engine fetches its models at build time
    Code licence MIT
    Component licences the default engine uses permissively licensed libraries; the optional layout-model engine adds model weights under Apache-2.0 and CDLA-Permissive-2.0; LibreOffice, which converts DOC and XLS and renders office documents, is listed. Each run lists the components it used, each with its own licence or terms. No licence of an output is computed: Licences and paradata
    Record SSH Open Marketplace tool jGxgQI

    Steps¶

    # Step What happens In → out Activity
    1 Read the file When an AMČR seed is sent, its source.sha512 is checked against the file's bytes first, and a mismatch is refused. The type is recognised from the content, and decides the workflow. Visual: a light engine reads a PDF on its own pages, and an office document as its own text placed on the pages of a LibreOffice rendering. Textual: a spreadsheet keeps every non-empty cell as the sheet displays it, and a text file its lines. file → pages, blocks, lines Converting
    2 Check the text layer Every page's text layer is tested: digital (it decodes), garbled (it does not), ocr (an earlier OCR run) or none (no text). A PDF page that is not digital is needsAtr, with a reason; an office page is needsAtr only by the PDF rule: no text of its own (none outside its header and footer) and a picture. A line that does not decode keeps the flag decode:garbled (decode:inverted for mirrored text): a verdict on its characters, apart from the line quality ocr-postprocess scores. pages → verdicts —
    3 Write the docproc Pages, blocks with their lines and boxes, headings, tables as cell grids, and the route of every page in metadata.routing: text-layer, atr or empty; a picture, chart or drawing is a figure block with its box. An empty page is routed empty and left out of pages[]. The docproc is validated against its schema; one that fails is never returned, and a limit that would cut content fails the job. → docproc (JSON), paradata Converting
    4 Render, when asked for The document is turned into annotated Markdown, page by page in reading order, with the layout carried in HTML comments: the one form in which a document is given to a language model, as in keyword-extract's whole-document runs. → Markdown —

    What you get¶

    The docproc: for every page, its size, its blocks in reading order with their lines and boxes, and its verdict in textLayer; for every page of the original, its route in metadata.routing. When no page needs the ATR, this docproc is the one stored for the document. When a page needs it, the ATR's docproc is the stored one, and this one is an intermediate that ocr-postprocess merges with it. An office document's text is what the file holds, read in full; one of its pages needs the ATR by the PDF rule, when it has no text of its own (none outside its header and footer) and shows a picture, such as a pasted scan, and the ATR then reads that page as it reads a PDF page. A picture, chart or drawing is a figure block with its box and nothing of the picture; with the forced pages, the pages with figure blocks are the ones page-classification reads. In a PDF only raster images are figures; a chart drawn as vectors is not. When the ATR or page-classification reads an office document, its rendered PDF comes with it; it is not stored, and every office document's paradata name it by its SHA-512, because its pages are the ones the docproc's page numbers and boxes refer to.

    Limits¶

    • Unruled tables are read by column, and letter-spaced captions and map labels split into pieces.
    • An office document's pages are LibreOffice's, which can break pages differently from Word; the docproc and the rendered PDF use the same pages.
    • Text inside a picture on an office page that has text of its own (a photo of a page beside a paragraph, a caption under a scan) is not read: the page keeps its text, and its figure block puts it among the pages for page-classification.
    • Spreadsheets and text files have no geometry: their pages and lines have no positions.
    • The converter was tried on 15 AMČR excavation reports, which showed its defects; there is no accuracy figure.

    Provenance and licence¶

    Every /reformat call returns the run's paradata: an RO-Crate CreateAction that names the file it read as original, the seed when one was sent, and the docproc it wrote, each by SHA-512; when a page needs the ATR the docproc is marked as not stored. On the command line, --paradata-dir writes a paradata file. The paradata list the components that ran, each with its licence, as declared in the tool's para_config.txt: with the default engine, permissively licensed libraries only, and the layout-model engine adds only permissive ones. LibreOffice, which converts DOC and XLS and renders office documents for their pages, is listed with its version whenever a run uses it, as a fact; the text stays the document's own. An office document's rendered PDF is named among the outputs by its digest, as a stored distribution, when it is handed over. No licence of an output is computed: the rights in an output follow the source document (Licences and paradata).

    The tool owns no block of the document record: it writes docproc, and ocr-postprocess, the record builder, writes the record from it. Records written by releases before 2.0.0-beta name the converter as digital-convert, the program id it keeps.

    Where it sits¶

    • The first step of a born-digital document — Pipelines → The route through the pipeline and W2. Scans and images go to the ATR instead. The AMČR pipeline calls /reformat, which calls no other service: the pipeline's orchestrator (Temporal) chains the steps.
    • Mixed documents — Pipelines → W3. When some pages need the ATR, the whole document goes through the ATR with those pages forced, and page-classification classifies those pages and the pages with figure blocks beside it; ocr-postprocess keeps this docproc's exact lines on the other pages.
    • What follows it. The record ocr-postprocess builds goes on to nlp-enrich and keyword-extract.
    • Born-digital extraction step of the AMČR text workflow 0xSpVP (step 6, beside GROBID).
    • The service image is one of the containerised services — Pipelines → W4.
